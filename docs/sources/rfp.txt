BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS
INTRODUCTION
JPS seeks proposals for an AI-enabled Utilization Management (UM) solution that integrates with Epic and related enterprise systems to support timely, consistent, and prioritized UM review across inpatient and observation encounters. The solution should analyze available clinical, utilization, payer, authorization, and encounter data to generate actionable, explainable recommendations for admission status review, continued-stay review, payer authorization needs, and physician advisor escalation. The goal is to improve review timeliness, status accuracy, regulatory compliance, revenue integrity, and throughput by moving UM workflows from primarily manual queue review to intelligent, risk-stratified prioritization.
BACKGROUND
The Tarrant County Hospital District, known as JPS Health Network (“District”), is a tax-supported organization serving the healthcare needs of families across Tarrant County. JPS provides adult inpatient care at John Peter Smith Hospital, a facility licensed for 582 beds and located in Fort Worth, Texas. JPS has served as a Level I Trauma Center for Tarrant County for over a decade and is currently the largest training institution in Fort Worth.

The health network offers comprehensive services including primary care, specialty care, and pharmacy at more than 25 community locations. JPS is dedicated to providing a full continuum of behavioral health services, including inpatient services at Trinity Springs Pavilion, emergency behavioral health services at our Psychiatric Emergency Center, outpatient services at our JPS clinics, and mental health services for children and adolescents through programs like TCHATT.
JPS is governed by an eleven (11) member Board of Managers, whose members are appointed by the Tarrant County Commissioners Court.
PROJECT SCOPE
JPS manages a high volume of inpatient and observation encounters across complex Medicare, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, managed care, and commercial payer requirements. Current UM workflows depend heavily on manual work queues and human triage to identify which encounters require immediate review, which cases are at risk for status mismatch, which observation patients require escalation, and which inpatient stays may require continued-stay or denial-prevention intervention. As admission volumes fluctuate and patient stays shorten, it can be difficult to consistently identify the highest-risk cases early enough in the hospitalization, and some encounters may reach discharge before the UM review process is fully completed.

Key opportunities for process improvement include earlier initial status assessment, more reliable prioritization of observation and short-stay encounters, proactive identification of payer authorization and notification requirements, streamlined physician advisor referral, and better operational visibility into UM work queue performance. JPS needs an AI-enabled UM platform that continuously analyzes relevant encounter data, identifies cases requiring urgent review or escalation, and provides explainable, criteria-aligned recommendations while preserving JPS clinical, regulatory, and operational oversight. The solution should support more consistent application of UM criteria, improved alignment between patient status and clinical need, reduced avoidable denials, and earlier resolution of status and authorization barriers that can affect patient progression.

This solution should meet the following goals: 
Improve timeliness and prioritization of initial UM review for new inpatient and observation encounters.
Support consistent, explainable, criteria-aligned recommendations for inpatient versus observation status and continued-stay review.
Identify encounters requiring physician advisor escalation, payer authorization action, or denial-prevention intervention earlier in the hospital stay.
Improve operational visibility through real-time dashboards, alerts, worklists, and performance reporting.
Strengthen compliance, revenue integrity, and throughput by reducing missed, delayed, or low-priority manual review activity.

The selected vendor shall provide, implement, integrate, and support an AI-enabled Utilization Management platform for JPS. The scope includes discovery, workflow design, technical integration, configuration, testing, training, go-live support, reporting, and ongoing optimization. The solution must support UM review workflows across initial admission review, observation management, continued-stay review, payer authorization and notification monitoring, denial-risk identification, and physician advisor escalation.
The platform must integrate with Epic and other relevant JPS systems to ingest timely encounter, ADT, order, clinical documentation, laboratory, imaging, medication, procedure, payer, authorization, and utilization data as applicable. The vendor must configure role-based workflows for UM reviewers, physician advisors, case management leaders, and operational leaders. The solution must present prioritized worklists, explainable AI-generated recommendations, source-data transparency, alerts, audit trails, and dashboards that allow users to understand why a case is prioritized and what action is recommended.
The vendor shall support payer-specific rules, regulatory requirements, and local workflow configuration. The solution must function as a decision-support tool and shall not autonomously change patient status, submit payer actions, or replace required clinical and regulatory review. Final determinations and external payer actions must remain under JPS-approved workflows.

MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS
System Architecture
The solution must be built on a cloud-based or cloud-ready enterprise architecture that supports scalability, high availability, secure deployment, and rapid configuration across UM workflows. The architecture should be API-first and support secure bidirectional integration with Epic, ADT feeds, clinical documentation, payer and authorization data, criteria tools, enterprise reporting tools, and other systems identified by JPS. The platform must support role-based access, single sign-on where applicable, audit logging, data lineage, model monitoring, configurable workflows, and reliable performance for near real-time operational use.
Integration
The solution must integrate with JPS’s existing technology environment, including Epic and relevant clinical, operational, financial, and payer-related systems, to provide a unified, near real-time view of encounters requiring UM action. Integrations should support admission status review, observation management, continued-stay review, payer authorization and notification tracking, denial-risk identification, physician advisor escalation, and reporting. Data exchange should support operational decision-making with sufficient timeliness for users to intervene before status, authorization, or review delays affect patient progression or revenue integrity.
Security
• The vendor must describe in detail how the solution protects data confidentiality, integrity, and availability across all environments, integrations, users, and workflows.
• The solution must fully comply with HIPAA and HITECH requirements for handling PHI and must support execution of a Business Associate Agreement with JPS.
• The solution must use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, least-privilege access, audit logging, secure authentication, and appropriate session management.
• The vendor must describe data retention, backup, disaster recovery, incident response, vulnerability management, penetration testing, and business continuity practices.

Implementing an AI-enabled Utilization Management solution would provide significant strategic, operational, clinical, compliance, and financial value to JPS. Expected value includes improved initial review timeliness, more consistent status recommendations, stronger observation management, earlier recognition of payer authorization risk, improved physician advisor prioritization, better denial prevention, and more transparent UM performance reporting. By aligning clinical data, payer requirements, UM criteria, and operational workflow in a single intelligent platform, JPS can strengthen compliance and revenue integrity while supporting patient progression through earlier resolution of status and authorization barriers.
